## Additional notice for hardware control

Ventus writes fan-control values to the System Management Controller (SMC) of
Apple Silicon Macs. It ships with layered safety measures — a control-loop
watchdog, a 95 °C thermal override, verified restoration to Apple's automatic
fan control, and release of control on sleep and on daemon exit — but hardware
control is inherently risky.

You run it at your own risk. As stated in the Disclaimer above, the Software is
provided without warranty of any kind and we accept no liability for damage to
your hardware.
